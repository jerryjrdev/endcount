3Volumes are estimates

Volumes in EndCount are estimates for receiving checks and inventory. They do not replace a scale ticket.

EndCount works out board feet and log scale volumes from the sizes you enter, using published formulas and log rule tables. Results depend on what you enter and on the rule you choose, and the same log gives a different number under each rule. Check important numbers yourself before you rely on them, for example before you sign for a load or settle a payment.
