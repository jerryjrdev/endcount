Privacy Policy

This policy explains what happens to your information when you use EndCount Board Foot Calculator (“EndCount”) on iPhone.

Effective 20 November 2026

The short version

  • There is no account and no sign-in.
  • Your records, photos and company logo stay on your iPhone. We can't see them.
  • Apple processes your payments. RevenueCat records your subscription status for us.
  • No ads, no tracking and no analytics tools. We never sell your information.

1Who we are

EndCount is published by QIDIENT GLOBAL LTD (“we”, “us”). We are responsible for the information described in this policy. You can reach us at support@qidientlabs.com.

2What stays on your iPhone

Everything you put into EndCount is stored in the app's own storage on your iPhone. That includes your receiving records and their lines, logs and edit history, supplier and ticket details, notes, prices, record photos and the marks you add to them, your company name and logo, species lists and your settings.

  • EndCount has no account and no server of its own. This information is never sent to us.
  • EndCount 1.0 does not sync with iCloud.
  • Because we never receive your records, we cannot see, change or recover them.
  • If you back up your iPhone with iCloud Backup or to a computer, that backup can include EndCount's data. Backups are handled by Apple or your computer under your own settings.

3Camera and photos

  • Camera. EndCount uses the camera only when you choose to take a record photo. iOS asks for your permission first, and you can change it at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Camera.
  • Choosing a photo. When you add a photo to a record or choose your company logo, you pick it in the iOS photo picker. EndCount receives only the photo you pick and cannot see the rest of your library.
  • Saving to Photos. EndCount adds a marked record photo to your photo library only when you tap Save Image. iOS asks for permission first.
  • Photo details removed. EndCount keeps only the picture itself. Location and camera details in a photo are not kept with your records, and they are not included in anything you export.

4Files you share

PDF reports, CSV files and marked images leave your iPhone only when you share them with the iOS share sheet, to the person, app or service you choose. A PDF report can include your company name and logo. Exported photos contain no location data or other photo details. After you share a file, how it is handled is up to the person or service that receives it.

5Purchases and subscriptions

Apple

EndCount Pro subscriptions are sold and billed by Apple through the App Store. Apple handles your payment details. We never see your card number or your Apple Account details. The app checks with Apple whether your subscription is active. Apple's handling of your information is described in Apple's Privacy Policy.

RevenueCat

We use RevenueCat, Inc. to record trials and subscriptions. When you start a trial, subscribe, renew, restore or cancel, the RevenueCat software in EndCount sends RevenueCat:

  • purchase and subscription information, such as the plan, price and currency, purchase, renewal and expiry dates, whether a trial is active, and App Store transaction identifiers;
  • an anonymous app user ID, a random identifier created by the app that is not tied to your name, email address or Apple Account;
  • basic device and app information, such as the iOS version, the app version, your language and App Store country. Like any internet service, RevenueCat also receives your IP address when the app connects to it.

We use this to keep an accurate record of whether your subscription or trial is active, and to see how trials and subscriptions are doing overall. RevenueCat handles this information on our behalf as our service provider. It never receives your records, photos or logo. You can read RevenueCat's Privacy Policy.

6When you email us

If you write to support@qidientlabs.com, we receive your email address and whatever you include in your message. We use it only to answer you.

7Crash reports from Apple

If you have turned on Share With App Developers in Settings > Privacy & Security > Analytics & Improvements, Apple may share crash reports and app usage statistics with us. Apple prepares these reports and they do not identify you. You control this setting, not EndCount.

8What we don't do

  • We don't show ads.
  • We don't track you across other companies' apps or websites, and we don't use the advertising identifier.
  • We don't use analytics, advertising or attribution software.
  • We don't access your location.
  • We don't sell your information or share it for advertising.

9Why we use information

Where data protection laws such as the GDPR or UK GDPR apply, we rely on these legal bases: subscription records are needed to provide the subscription you bought (performance of a contract) and to keep accurate business records (our legitimate interests); support emails are used to answer you (our legitimate interests); and some records must be kept to meet tax and accounting rules (legal obligation).

10How long information is kept, and how to delete it

  • On your iPhone: until you delete it. To delete a record, swipe it in Records and tap Delete; its photos and lines are removed. To remove your logo, open Settings > Logo and tap Remove logo. Deleting the EndCount app removes all of its data from your iPhone. Copies in earlier iPhone backups stay until those backups are deleted.
  • Subscription records: kept by RevenueCat while your subscription is active, and afterwards for as long as needed for accounting, tax and legal reasons. Apple keeps its own purchase records.
  • Support emails: kept only as long as needed to help you and keep a record of the conversation.

To ask us to delete or send you a copy of the subscription records or emails we hold about you, write to support@qidientlabs.com. Because EndCount doesn't know your name or email address, please include the order ID from your App Store receipt email so we can find the right record. We may need to keep some records where the law requires it.

11Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to access, correct or delete your personal information, to object to or restrict how it is used, and to receive a copy of it. You can also complain to your local data protection authority. Email us to use any of these rights and we will answer within the time the law requires. We will not treat you differently for using them.

12Children

EndCount is a work tool for lumber yards, sawmills and woodworkers. It is not directed to children under 13, or the minimum age in your country, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has sent us personal information, email us and we will delete it.

13International transfers

Apple and RevenueCat may process information in the United States and other countries, which may have different data protection laws from the country where you live. Where the law requires it, these transfers are protected by safeguards such as the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses.

14Security

Your records are protected by iOS app isolation and by your iPhone's passcode and data protection. Connections to Apple and RevenueCat are encrypted.

15Changes to this policy

We will update this policy when the app or the law changes, for example if a later version of EndCount adds optional iCloud sync. We will post the new version on this page and change the effective date. If a change affects how your information is handled in a significant way, we will also say so in the App Store release notes.
