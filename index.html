EndCount support

EndCount Board Foot Calculator does the board foot and log scale math on iPhone for lumber yard receiving, sawmills and woodworkers. Keep a running tally of each load and save it as a receiving record with photos and a PDF report.

Contact support

support@qidientlabs.com

We reply within 2 business days. Please include your iPhone model, your iOS version and the EndCount version (shown at the bottom of Settings in the app), and tell us what happened.

Questions

  • How do I restore my subscription?

    Make sure your iPhone is signed in with the Apple Account you subscribed with. Then open EndCount and tap Restore Purchases at the bottom of the subscription screen, or go to Settings > Restore purchases in the app.

    Restoring brings back your subscription, not your records. Records are stored on the iPhone they were made on. They move to a new iPhone when you transfer your data with Quick Start or restore the new iPhone from a backup of the old one.

  • How do I manage or cancel my subscription?

    On your iPhone, open Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions and choose EndCount. You can also go to Settings > Manage subscription inside EndCount.

    To avoid being charged for the next period, cancel at least 24 hours before the current period (or your trial) ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription. Apple handles all billing and refunds; you can ask Apple for a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.

  • How does the 7-day free trial work?

    New subscribers can try EndCount Pro Weekly or EndCount Pro Yearly for 7 days at no charge. There is one trial per Apple Account, shared by both plans.

    When the trial ends, the plan you chose starts and its price is charged to your Apple Account: $13.99 per week or $130.99 per year in the US (prices vary by region and are shown before you confirm). If you don't want to continue, cancel in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions at least 24 hours before the trial ends.

  • I subscribed, but EndCount asks me to subscribe again

    Check that your iPhone uses the same Apple Account you subscribed with, then tap Restore Purchases. You can see whether the subscription is active in Settings > Apple Account > Subscriptions. If a subscription has ended, your records are still on your iPhone and open again when you renew.

  • Where is my data stored?

    On your iPhone. Your records, photos, company logo, species prices and settings are kept in EndCount's own storage on the device. There is no account and no EndCount server, and EndCount 1.0 does not sync with iCloud. We can't see or recover your records, so keep your iPhone backed up.

    Apple processes subscription payments, and RevenueCat records subscription status for us. The Privacy Policy has the details.

  • How do I delete my data?

    • One record: in Records, swipe the record and tap Delete. Its photos and lines are removed from your iPhone.
    • Your company name and logo: in EndCount's Settings, clear the company name, then open Logo and tap Remove logo.
    • Everything: delete the EndCount app (touch and hold its icon, then Remove App > Delete App). Any iPhone backups you made earlier may still hold a copy.

    To ask us to delete subscription records kept by RevenueCat, or emails you sent us, write to support@qidientlabs.com.

  • Are the volumes exact?

    Every volume in EndCount is an estimate from the sizes you enter. Log totals name the rule they use (Doyle, Scribner Decimal C or International 1/4), and the same log gives a different number under each rule. In the app, Settings > How estimates work explains the math.

    Estimates from the sizes you enter, for receiving checks, tallies and inventory. EndCount does not replace a scale ticket.
